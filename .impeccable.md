## Design Context

### Users
Cebu students, developers, designers, writers, first-time contributors, volunteers, and community organizers. They need to understand Hacktoberfest, find the October gatherings, see real local work, and find a way to participate. Context comes from the user's accepted BRIEF.md and subsequent feedback.

### Brand Personality
Welcoming, resourceful, collective. Speak as another community member. Be specific and truthful about event readiness.

### Aesthetic Direction
Continue the Open Assembly mockup: warm paper, near-black, vermilion, moss, real archival photography, bold type, assembled geometric elements. Use sturdier, more readable display type and direct tech-event language. No generic SaaS cards, fabricated activity, or invented partners.

### Design Principles
1. Let real Cebu people and projects carry the identity.
2. Keep past editions visible and label their dates.
3. Make the next useful action obvious and functional.
4. Build for small screens, keyboards, and reduced motion.
5. Prefer durable content and small interactions over ongoing operations.
